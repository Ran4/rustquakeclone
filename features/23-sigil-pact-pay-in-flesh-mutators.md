# 23. The Sigil Pact — Pay-in-Flesh Mutators

> At every exit slipgate, three sigils offer to make you stronger — and the next dimension darker — so the eight hand-built maps re-roll into a personalised, escalating curse.

**The idea** — When you reach the exit, the slipgate doesn't just swallow you: it floats three glowing **sigils**, and you must touch one to pass. Each is a Faustian trade — a buff *to you* paid for by a debuff *to the world you're about to enter*. "Bloodthirst": +25% damage, but the next level's fog clamps in 20m closer. "Iron Skin": flat damage soak, but a second Death Knight stalks the map. "Wraithstep": faster air-accel, but the hazard creeps — lava pools widen and tick harder each level it's stacked. The pact compounds: by level 8 a greedy player is fighting a fog-choked, Knight-infested dam with the lava licking up the piers.

**Why it's fresh** — Roguelite mutators usually swap rulesets at a menu. Here the choice happens *diegetically at the slipgate*, the cost is paid by the very next themed map mutating around your decision, and because debuffs **stack across all eight levels**, every run authors its own difficulty curve. It turns the fixed campaign into a personalised escalation without touching a single hand-built brush.

**How it plays** — Each exit becomes a real decision under fatigue: do I bank power now and gamble that I can survive a darker level 7, or play it safe and arrive weak? You learn which debuffs *you* can eat — a fog reader takes the fog, a kiter declines the extra Knight. The sigils are visible the moment you spot the exit, so you're weighing the pact while still clearing the room.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It's almost pure data injection into the existing pipeline. `gamestate.rs`'s `exit_system` already snapshots the loadout into `RunState` before bouncing through `Loading`; the chosen sigils ride alongside it as a `Vec<Sigil>`. `setup_level`/`apply_theme_and_build` in `level.rs` reads the theme into `LevelStyle` (`fog_end`, `hazard_dot`, `hazard_emissive`) *before* dispatching `build_index`, and fills `SpawnPlan.monsters` after — exactly the two seams a mutator pass needs: stomp the style fields, then append a Death Knight to `plan.monsters` and widen `LavaVolumes`. Buffs live in the player/combat tunables read by `weapons.rs`, `combat.rs` and `physics.rs`. The slipgate sigils are three more hand-built low-poly pickups in the `pickups.rs` style.

**Build sketch** — Define a `Sigil` enum and a small applicator. Render the trio at the exit; a touch records it in `RunState`. After `apply_theme_and_build` reads the theme but before/after `build_index`, run an `apply_sigils` pass that mutates `LevelStyle`, `SpawnPlan` and `LavaVolumes`, and a player-buff pass on spawn. The honest hard part is **balance and legibility**: stacked debuffs must stay survivable and the player must always understand *why* the dam is suddenly drowning in lava — needs a persistent "active pacts" HUD line and careful caps per debuff.

**Effort** — **M.** Plumbing is small and rides proven seams; the risk is tuning the compounding curve so a greedy run is brutal-but-fair rather than an unwinnable level 8.

# 03. Telefrag Nail — The Nailgun Blink-Dash

> Hold the Nailgun to spit a glowing beacon-nail, then tap again to blink your AABB to wherever it stuck — velocity and all — across gaps no jump could clear.

**The idea** — Charge the Nailgun (hold fire) instead of spraying, and it launches a single bright *beacon nail* that flies, drops under gravity, and sticks wherever it first hits: a wall, a Death Knight's flak plate, or the deck of the level-8 truck. A second tap on the alt-fire teleports the player to that anchor. It is not a freezing blink — your current velocity is preserved and re-applied at the destination, so a beacon thrown across a void rift lets you arrive mid-sprint and keep air-strafing. The beacon decays after a few seconds (or one use), so you can't hoard infinite escape hatches.

**Why it's fresh** — Quake's telefragging was an accident of map design; here it's a *deliberate traversal verb*. Crucially, the beacon is sticky to dynamic surfaces — plant it on the moving truck or on a fleeing monster, drive/fight elsewhere, then snap back. A velocity-carrying teleport that resolves through the same swept solver as normal movement (rather than a hard set-position) is rare even in modern shooters.

**How it plays** — A new economy of *deferred positioning*. Tag a high ledge, drop down to grab the Silver Key, blink back up. Beacon the slipgate side of a void rift, then fight on the near side knowing your exit is one tap away. Lob a beacon onto the truck bed as it rolls past and blink aboard at speed. The skill ceiling is reading arcs, timing the decay, and chaining a blink out of a wall-jump to bridge two air-strafe lines.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It extends `weapons.rs` (a charge state on the existing Nailgun) and `projectiles.rs` (`ProjKind::Nail` gains a beacon variant). The teleport runs through `physics.rs` — `segment_aabb` validates the destination AABB is clear (telefrag a monster overlapping it via the same overlap test, mirroring splash logic in `combat.rs`), then a zero-length `move_and_slide` depenetrates and settles you, carrying `vel`. Sticking to the truck reuses `vehicle.rs`'s reserved `WorldColliders` slot: store the beacon as an offset in the truck's local frame and re-resolve world-space each frame, exactly like the moving-brush collider. Hit feedback leans on `effects.rs` muzzle/explosion lights and a synth blink-whoosh in `audio_gen.rs`.

**Build sketch** — Add a charge timer and beacon-spawn to the Nailgun; give the beacon nail an `on-stick` that records its anchor (world point, or entity + local offset for movers). The blink: snapshot velocity, build the destination AABB at the anchor, overlap-test for telefraggable monsters (apply lethal damage + gibs), then depenetrate and re-seat the player through the solver. The honest hard part is the *moving anchor*: a beacon on the truck must track its yaw/translation per-frame and survive the truck despawning between levels — and a blink into a wall-flush mover must reject or shove cleanly, not embed and freeze the swept solver.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: the dynamic-anchor + velocity-carry edge cases (teleporting onto or off a moving deck mid-spin) producing penetration or out-of-bounds blinks; mitigated by reusing the existing depenetrate-then-slide path and rejecting blinks whose destination fails the clearance test.

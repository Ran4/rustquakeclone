# 14. Powder Keg — Throwable, Rideable, Rammable Barrel

> A black-powder barrel you can surf down a ramp, Whip-punt across a room, or truck-roll into a crowd — then shoot its fuse mid-roll to set off a daisy-chaining cluster bomb.

**The idea** — A waist-high wooden keg that is, simultaneously, every physics object QUAKECLONE already knows how to be: a moving solid you can stand on (like the truck deck), a loose body that takes pushes (like a gibbed monster), and a hazard that detonates (like a grenade). Whip it, shoot it, rocket-splash it, ram it with the truck, or just hop on and ride it down a slope. Land a shot on the **fuse band** and it lights — a few seconds of rolling, hissing menace — then a hefty radius blast that flings (and ignites the fuse of) every other keg nearby.

**Why it's fresh** — Barrels in shooters are static red props that pop. This one is a *vehicle, a projectile, and a bomb in one object*, and the three modes interact: a ridden keg you've punted is a moving platform that's also a live grenade. Chain-reaction kegs turn a corridor into a Newton's-cradle of fire — a player-authored Rube Goldberg you trigger with one well-placed pellet.

**How it plays** — New verbs stack onto the old ones. Whip-punt a keg down a ramp into a Knight pack, then snipe its fuse for an airburst. Surf one across a lava channel as a rolling stepping-stone. Truck-ram a stack to bowl them into a Death Knight. The skill is *timing the fuse shot* — too early wastes the roll, too late and it blows under your own feet. Risk is symmetric: rammed or splashed kegs roll back at *you*.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It leans almost entirely on parts that exist. The keg reserves a `WorldColliders` slot and rewrites it each frame from its transform — the exact moving-brush trick `vehicle.rs` and the doors use — so player collision, monster pathing, hitscan and LoS treat it as solid for free. It carries a velocity + angular velocity and bounces off `physics.rs`'s swept-AABB wall normals with restitution, reusing the truck's crash code. Rider-carry slides whoever stands on it. Whip knockback (`weapons.rs`), rocket splash (`projectiles.rs`) and `combat.rs` damage all push it via the same impulse path. Detonation just fires an `ExplosionEvent` (`common.rs`) — pos/radius/damage/push already wired into `combat.rs`. Authored through the `level.rs` Build API as a `keg(pos)` prop; fuse hiss and the wood-crack pop synthesised in `audio_gen.rs`; barrels survive level exit checks in `gamestate.rs`.

**Build sketch** — A `Keg` component holding velocity, angular velocity and an optional fuse timer; a per-frame system that integrates motion through `move_and_slide`, applies restitution/spin on the returned normal (lifted from the truck), and rewrites the reserved collider slot. Pushes from Whip/splash/ram feed one `apply_impulse` helper. A fuse-lit keg ticks down, and on zero (or on a direct hit) emits an `ExplosionEvent`; chain reactions fall out for free, since the blast's `push` re-impulses neighbours and a small radius-overlap check lights their fuses. The honest hard part is the **rolling-platform feel**: a keg is a cylinder but our solver is AABB-only, so it must fake roll as visual mesh spin over a box collider, and rider-carry on a *tumbling* surface (vs. the truck's yaw-only body) needs care to avoid launching the player on every bounce.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: rideable + rolling + explosive at once is a tuning minefield — chain reactions can cascade into a frame-spike or fling the player unpredictably, so blast push, fuse timing and the AABB-roll fudge all need clamps and playtesting.

# 53. Style Engine — The Announcer Rates Your Carnage

> Shotgun, shotgun, shotgun is boring. Rocket-jump, whip-parry, ram, lightning — now the announcer is screaming.

**The idea** — A Devil-May-Cry-style carnage meter sits over the whole fight: a `Style` resource that climbs through ranks (D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS) and a screamed announcer that escalates with it. Every kill scores points, but *repeating* the same weapon or the same trick pays out less and less, while *varied* and *risky* kills pay big — an airborne frag, a truck ram, a rocket-jump kill, a whip-parry into a finisher, a no-damage streak. The rank decays steadily and *crashes* the instant you take a hit or grind one button, so the meter only stays lit while you keep mixing the toolbox. High rank is a tangible reward, not just a number: faster health regen, a louder, denser soundtrack layer, and a per-level carnage score on the exit banner.

**Why it's fresh** — Style ranks are a character-action staple but almost unheard of in a Quake-flavoured shooter, and they fit Quake's whole toolbox-of-overlapping-verbs design perfectly. Nothing else turns "use every weapon and movement trick" into a live, audible feedback loop, and pinning the rank to *variety and risk* — not raw damage — reframes the entire arsenal as an expression set rather than a DPS spreadsheet.

**How it plays** — You stop spamming the Super Shotgun and start choreographing: pop a Scrag in mid-air, whip-shove a Knight off a ledge, rocket-jump into a clustered lightning kill, all before the meter cools. The announcer climbing from a flat "Dull" to a shrieked "SSStylish!" is pure dopamine, and the no-damage clause makes greedy aggression a genuine gamble — one careless hit and your SSS drops to D.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — The meter feeds off events that already exist: `combat.rs` is the single chokepoint for kills, `DamageEvent`, armor absorb and death, so the `Style` resource (owned by `gamestate.rs`, reset per level like the objective) just subscribes to those. `weapons.rs` already knows which of the seven weapons fired the killing blow, and `player.rs` already tracks `Player.vel`, the grounded/airborne flag and the rocket-jump/wall-jump state — those tag a kill as airborne, ram (via `vehicle.rs`'s pulp-at-speed path) or rocket-jump for the variety bonus. `hud.rs` draws the rank letter and its draining bar alongside the existing crosshair/objective tracker, and `audio_gen.rs` synthesises the announcer stings and a music-intensity layer scrubbed live exactly like the truck engine's `AudioSink` — composing cleanly with the Adrenal Conductor heartbeat (idea 11) by sharing the same per-frame volume scrub. The regen payoff is a simple multiplier on the health logic in `gamestate.rs`.

**Build sketch** — Add a `Style` resource holding rank, points, a decay timer and a per-weapon/per-trick recency map; on each kill event, award points scaled down by how recently that weapon-or-trick last scored, add risk bonuses from the player flags, and reset decay; drain every frame and hard-crash on `DamageEvent` to the player. The honest hard part is the *scoring rules* — they must reward genuine variety and punish a degenerate optimum, which wants playtesting and telemetry — plus authoring announcer stings that hype without grating (short, sparse, rate-limited, and duckable under the heartbeat).

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: scoring that rewards real variety instead of a degenerate loop, and announcer audio that excites rather than grates.

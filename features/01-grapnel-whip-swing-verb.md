# 01. Grapnel Whip — Weapon 7 Becomes a Swing Verb

> The Whip already throws monsters back — now its alt-fire throws *you* forward, biting into brickwork and slinging you across the gap.

**The idea** — Hold right-mouse with the Whip out and it fires the same crack, but instead of a melee shove it casts a hitscan line that latches onto solid world geometry. The latch anchors a rope; the player is reeled toward the anchor while gravity pulls down, so you arc through the air on a taut pendulum. Let go and you keep all the momentum the swing built — release at the bottom of the arc to fly far and flat, release late to launch upward. Anchor onto a ceiling and it's a vertical yank; anchor onto a far ledge mid-jump and it's a Spider-Man crossing. The Whip's existing melee left-click is untouched.

**Why it's fresh** — Quake never had a grapple in the retail game (only the mission packs / mods did), and almost no shooter fuses a grapple with *air-strafe* movement. Here the rope constraint and the air-accel cap compose: you can air-strafe *while* swinging to pump the pendulum wider, a skill that exists in neither pure-rope games nor pure-Quake. It's a traversal verb grown from a melee weapon, not a bolted-on gadget.

**How it plays** — A new spatial read of every room: that lava channel on level 1, the molten-metal void on level 3's catwalks, the obsidian-island gaps of level 7 all become swingable instead of deadly. Decisions multiply — anchor high for height or low for speed, release early or ride the arc, chain a wall-jump out of a swing's apex. Skilled players will rocket-jump *into* a grapple to keep a chain of momentum alive.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It reuses `physics.rs::raycast_world` (already returns the hit point + wall normal the swing needs) for the latch, and writes back into the same `PlayerMove.vel` that `player.rs` air-accel, gravity and wall-jump already mutate. The rope is just a per-frame velocity constraint layered before the existing `move_and_slide` swept solver, so collisions, step-up and slides keep working untouched. It can anchor onto the `vehicle.rs` moving-brush collider for free (grapple the moving truck). The crack SFX comes straight from the Whip's `audio_gen.rs` entry; a taut-rope loop is one more synth.

**Build sketch** — Cast the latch ray, store an anchor point and rope length. Each frame, if the player is farther than the rope length, project velocity onto the sphere surface (cancel the outward component) so they swing rather than stretch; let gravity and air-accel do the rest, then hand the result to the swept solver as usual. The honest hard part is *feel*: rope stiffness, whether to allow reel-in (shortening length over time), and keeping the constraint from fighting `move_and_slide` when the player scrapes a wall mid-swing — that wants careful ordering and a little damping, plus a clean auto-detach when the anchor breaks line-of-sight.

**Effort** — **M**. Main risk is tuning the pendulum so it feels like momentum, not a yo-yo on a stick; the math is small but the game-feel iteration loop is the real cost.

# 42. The Mimic — The Medkit Bites Back

> The only safe thing in Quake was the loot. Not any more.

**The idea** — The Mimic is a rare monster that wears a pickup as a costume. It sits *perfectly still* on the floor in the exact pose of a health globe, an armour shard or an ammo box — same little hand-built low-poly model, same glow — and waits. The instant the player crosses its trigger radius (the reflex range where you'd reach out and grab loot) the prop *blooms*: its facets unfold into a small, fast bone rig that rears up, lunges for an opening ambush bite, then settles into a normal chase. A fair, subtle tell separates it from the real thing — the glow is a half-shade off, and it never does the gentle idle bob that genuine pickups do — so a sharp player can read the trap and bait the lunge instead of eating it.

**Why it's fresh** — Every shooter trains you that the loot is the one safe object in the room; the Mimic poisons that single un-punished reflex. Quake never had a monster that *was* a pickup, and because here a pickup and a monster are both procedurally-assembled facet models from the same pipeline, the disguise is structurally honest rather than a swapped prefab — the thing you reach for is literally the thing that bites you.

**How it plays** — Suddenly every globe in a dim room is a question, and the answer costs either a beat of hesitation or a chunk of health. You learn the tell, then you learn to *exploit* it — sidle to the trigger edge, provoke the bloom, and gib the half-unfolded rig at its most vulnerable. It makes scavenging a deliberate act instead of an autopilot, and it makes a real pickup feel earned.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — The disguise is a real prop straight from `pickups.rs` (its little low-poly health/armour/ammo model), so a dormant Mimic *is* a pickup until triggered. The bloom hands off to `monster_model.rs`, whose `BoneSpec`/`Bone` assembler grows the prop's facets into a compact rig and runs the per-frame animator for the lunge swing; from there `enemies.rs` drives a new **Ambush -> Chase -> Attack** state machine that uses the standard line-of-sight perception once it's up. `combat.rs` handles the bite `DamageEvent`, armour absorb and the eventual death/gibs, `physics.rs` `move_and_slide` carries the small rig and `effects.rs` sells the unfold with particles and a tiny screen shake. The `QC_ITEMSHOT` debug path already proves a pickup model and a monster rig render together, so it doubles as the harness for tuning the morph.

**Build sketch** — Spawn a dormant entity that reuses the pickup mesh plus a `Mimic` marker and a trigger AABB; each frame, overlap-test the player (the same cheap test `gamestate.rs` hazards use) and on entry swap the static prop for the assembled `monster_model.rs` rig, kicking the AI into Ambush. The honest hard part is the visual morph from prop to rig reading as one continuous "unfold" rather than a pop, and tuning the tell — glow-shade delta and missing idle bob — so it's a fair read at fighting distance, not a coin-flip.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: making the prop-to-rig bloom look continuous and tuning the tell so spotting a Mimic is skill, not luck.

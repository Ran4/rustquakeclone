# 37. Wall-Run — Hold the Wall and Sprint the Skybox

> The wall-jump was a tap. Now hold it: run flat along the brickwork while the floor is just a rumour below.

**The idea** — While you carry speed and lean into a wall, you don't bounce off it — you *run along* it, sprinting horizontally (and a touch upward) across the brickwork for a short stamina window. Gravity along the wall is partly cancelled so you stick to the face instead of peeling away, and a fresh along-the-wall acceleration shoves you forward in the direction you're already travelling. Hold it and you blur across a span the player could never cross on foot; release, or run the stamina dry, and you peel off keeping every scrap of momentum you built. It's a *sustained* traversal verb, deliberately distinct from — and additive to — the existing mid-air wall-jump tap.

**Why it's fresh** — Quake gave you the wall-jump as a single explosive kick; nobody held the wall and *ran* it. Sustained wall-running is a Titanfall/Mirror's Edge verb almost never grafted onto a Quake-physics base, and dropping it beside bunny-hopping and air-strafing yields combos the genre has never paired: wall-run to build a horizontal line, wall-jump off its end for height, then whip-grapple out of the apex. The skybox stops being a backdrop and becomes a route.

**How it plays** — A new locomotion state slots between grounded and airborne: pressing into a wall at speed latches you flat to it and the world streaks past sideways. The decisions are all about timing — when to drop off for a height-trading wall-jump versus riding to the wall's end for raw distance, and which wall to commit to when two faces flank a void. Level 7's obsidian islands and the level 8 dam face turn from fall hazards into a connected horizontal playground.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It rides the systems the wall-jump already uses. Each frame `player.rs` calls `raycast_world` in `physics.rs` to find the adjacent wall and reuse its `wall_normal` and adjacency check — the same probe that gates the existing tap-to-kick. Instead of an instant impulse it adds an along-the-wall acceleration inside the established air-accel and air-strafe-cap framework and projects `GRAVITY` onto the wall plane, writing the result straight to `Player.vel`, then hands the velocity to `move_and_slide` exactly as normal locomotion does. A scuffing footfall loop synthesised in `audio_gen.rs` fades in with wall-run speed, and because nothing new touches the collider list it works against every `WorldColliders.solids` brush `level.rs` already emits — including the moving truck deck from `vehicle.rs`.

**Build sketch** — Add a `WallRun` band to the move code: when a wall is adjacent, speed is above a threshold and the stick key holds, kill the gravity component normal to the face, keep and amplify the tangent, and tick a stamina timer that forces a release. The honest hard part is feel plus seams — keeping the latch from snagging on convex brush corners where the swept solver hands back two competing normals, so you flow across a wall join instead of stuttering off it.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: dialing the stick/release thresholds for feel without snagging on convex brush seams in the swept solver.

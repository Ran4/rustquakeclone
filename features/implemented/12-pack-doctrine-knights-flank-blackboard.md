# 12. Pack Doctrine — Knights That Flank on a Blackboard

> Knights stop charging in a clump and start thinking as a squad: one bait holds your stare while the others read your blind arc off a shared blackboard and close the pincer.

**The idea** — When two or more Knights (or a Knight pack salted with an Ogre) share a room, a per-squad *blackboard* assigns roles instead of letting each monster solo-charge the player. One Knight becomes the **baiter** — it advances head-on into your view and trades hits to hold your gaze. The others become **flankers**: each picks an approach that keeps it *outside your facing cone* and routes toward your flank or back. A held-back **reserve** loiters in cover and only commits to a charge once two squadmates are already inside melee, so you can never burn the whole pack down in one corridor funnel.

**Why it's fresh** — Quake monsters (and QUAKECLONE's today) each run an identical independent `Idle→Chase→Attack` loop; "difficulty" is just more bodies. A blackboard makes the *group* the unit of intelligence: roles are claimed competitively, re-bid when a Knight dies, and computed from the player's own view direction. It's emergent pincer tactics out of brushwork the level author already laid, with zero navmesh.

**How it plays** — You can no longer hold a doorway and click. Facing the baiter means a flanker is sliding into your back arc; spinning to it frees the baiter to lunge. You're forced to use movement you already own — bunny-hop to a wall you can keep at your back, wall-jump out of a forming pincer, rocket-jump onto a ledge that collapses two flank lanes into one. Reading *which* Knight is the bait (it telegraphs by closing slower and squarer) becomes the new skill.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — Built almost entirely from parts already in the tree. Reverse-line-of-sight reuses `physics.rs` `line_of_sight`/`raycast_world` against `WorldColliders.solids` (which already folds in moving brushes from `vehicle.rs`/doors, so flankers respect the truck). "In your blind arc" is a dot of the flanker's bearing against the player camera yaw. The blackboard is a per-frame-rebuilt `Resource` in the spirit of `PlayerHistory` in `player.rs`. Roles only steer the existing `wish` vector and strafe in `enemies.rs` `enemy_ai`; no new movement, collision, or rig work — `monster_model.rs` animates it for free.

**Build sketch** — Add a `SquadBlackboard` resource keyed by spawn cluster (group Knights by proximity at `spawn_monsters`, or tag a squad id on `MonsterSpawn`). Each fixed tick: gather awake members, assign one baiter (nearest with clear LoS), bid the rest as flankers toward left/right flank slots chosen to stay outside the player's view cone, and gate the reserve on an "engaged count ≥ 2" flag. Feed the chosen slot back as the Knight's `wish` target. **The hard part** is making flank intent *legible* without true pathfinding — a flanker that walks into a wall reads as dumb, not clever; mitigate with a few raycast probes to pick a wall-free bearing and fall back to plain Chase when boxed in.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: flankers looking confused in tight, concave brush rooms where no clean flank lane exists; validate with `QC_AUTOTEST` and a 3-Knight test arena before shipping to the campaign.

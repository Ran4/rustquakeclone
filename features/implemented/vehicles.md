Add vehicel support.

A vehicle is a model that is placed in the world, that you can control.

It's to work like in half-life: you stand on a certain place in the vehicle, and if you do, you can press
E to activate it. If you have activated it, WASD goes towards controlling the vehicle (W to gas, S to break
and reverse, A/D to turn left/right).

You (or a monster!) should be able to stand on the vehicle and stay on it due to friction. So if you
jump into a vehicle, then start driving, then press e, you'll still remain on the vehicle (unless it's
going to fast that you're sliding away).

Create the vehicle system, and the initial vehicle - a truck with a flatbed.

The cabin should be open, and you should be able to jump into the flatbed.
The flatbed is just flat, no sidewalls - friction is what keeps yourself on the car.

Spawn the truck in front of the player on level 8 (do not spawn the truck in the other levels).

# 46. The Bed Gun — A Pintle Turret on the Flatbed

> Stop using the truck as a battering ram and start using it as a gun platform. Floor it, then man the deck cannon.

**The idea** — Bolt a pintle-mounted swivel cannon to the flatbed deck — a second mountable spot, taken with **E** exactly like the driver seat. Behind it you crew a heavy, slow-cycling, high-knockback cannon that you aim *independently* of the truck's heading while the wheels roll on momentum, a coast, or (in co-op fantasy) a passenger driving. Solo, the loop is: floor the truck toward the fight, leap to the gun spot, and bombard the room while the deck drifts under you on its own carried velocity. The truck stops being a one-trick ram and becomes a mobile weapons platform you can re-crew on the fly.

**Why it's fresh** — Quake never had a manned vehicle weapon, and FPS turrets are almost always static emplacements or rail-shooter mounts. Here the turret rides a *physics* deck that translates, spins, bounces off walls and gets flung by crashes — so your firing solution is layered on top of live momentum you set up a second ago. Aiming a slow cannon from a drifting, yawing platform is a genuinely new skill, and the hand-off between wheel and gun is a tactical beat no other dimension offers.

**How it plays** — You commit to a run-up, then gamble: keep driving and ram, or sacrifice the steering to coast and shell a crowd from the moving bed. The cannon's slow cycle and brutal knockback make every shot a decision — a hit pulps and flings, a miss leaves you a passenger on an unguided truck. Reading the deck's drift and the gun's lead at the same time is the whole game; corner too hot beforehand and the platform you wanted is now spinning out from under your shots.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — It is almost pure recombination of `vehicle.rs`: that module already has a mountable driver spot, `ActiveVehicle`, and the per-frame rider-carry that slides whoever stands on the translating-and-rotating deck — so a second gun spot is the same `E`-mount plumbing pointed at a new seat. The cannon is registered as a `weapons.rs` weapon firing a `projectiles.rs` shell with `combat.rs` radius splash, falloff and knockback, while the muzzle flash, point-light, screen shake (`effects.rs`) and `audio_gen.rs` report already exist for the existing guns. The truck still owns its reserved `WorldColliders` slot rewritten each frame, so `move_and_slide`, monster pathing, hitscan and LoS keep treating the bed as solid for free. The whole prop drops into a `levels/` map via the `level.rs` Build API beside the spawn-terrace truck.

**Build sketch** — Add a `GunSpot` mount alongside the driver spot in `vehicle.rs`; on mount, suppress the player's normal weapon view-model and bind fire to the cannon, deriving the muzzle world-transform from the deck transform times a local pintle offset so the barrel rides every translation and yaw. Shots feed the standard `ExplosionEvent`/projectile path; the rider-carry already keeps the gunner aboard. The honest hard part is the aim transform on a body that both translates and rotates each frame, plus a clean mode swap between drive and gun (and what the truck does while nobody holds the wheel).

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: a turret aim transform that rides a translating-and-rotating deck, and switching cleanly between drive and gun modes.

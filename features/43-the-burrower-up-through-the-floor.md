# 43. The Burrower — It Comes Up Through the Floor

> You hear the tremor and see the dust line racing at you across the floor. Then the floor opens.

**The idea** — The Burrower is a monster that spends most of its life *under the brushwork*, invisible, hunting you by your XZ position alone. In its submerged phase it surfaces nothing but a travelling mound — a rumbling tremor, a dust trail that chases your feet and ignores every wall and door between you. When it has closed the gap it **erupts directly under you**: a brief solid surfacing block heaves up and bumps you, the rig unfolds out of the ground, it lands one or two strikes, then **dives back down** and resubmerges to reposition for the next ambush. You never get a clean standing fight; you get tremors, eruptions, and the choice of where to be standing when the next one lands.

**Why it's fresh** — Every QUAKECLONE monster fights by line of sight — it has to *see* you to chase. The Burrower inverts that completely: while buried it cannot be seen *or* shot, knows your position through solid geometry, and surfaces only to commit. No FPS monster in this game defeats the LoS read, and a foe that arrives *vertically through the floor* turns the level's safe ground itself into the threat.

**How it plays** — The fight becomes about reading the dust line and refusing to stand still on the spot it's racing toward — keep moving, bait an eruption, then punish the brief window while the rig is surfaced and committed. Splash and the truck shine here: a rocket or a ram lands hard on a half-breached Burrower that can't dodge mid-eruption. Stand and reload over the tremor and you eat the ambush full in the back.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — The surfacing block is the **moving-collider trick** lifted straight from `vehicle.rs` and the sliding doors: reserve one `WorldColliders.solids` slot and rewrite it each frame from the mound's transform, so the eruption genuinely heaves the player off the spot via the existing `move_and_slide` carry, and submerges by collapsing that AABB to nothing. `enemies.rs` gains a `Submerged` ⇄ `Surfaced` cycle that replaces the LoS-gated `Chase` with a buried homing phase steering on the player's XZ, leaving the `Surfaced` strike to reuse the normal Attack swing. `monster_model.rs` drives a rig that folds flat while buried and unfolds as it breaches; `effects.rs` supplies the dust `particles` and `screen shake` on the eruption, and `audio_gen.rs` synthesises the looping subterranean rumble whose volume scrubs with proximity, like the truck engine.

**Build sketch** — Move the buried entity with a simple homing integrator that ignores `WorldColliders` entirely (it's underground), emit a dust-trail emitter pinned to its XZ on the floor plane, and trigger eruption when it reaches the player; on surface, spawn the moving collider, run the unfold + Attack, then retract and resubmerge on a timer. The honest hard part is *fair telegraphing* — the dust line and rumble must give a real dodge window — and making a geometry-ignoring buried entity feel like a hunter, not a cheat that teleports under you.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: tuning the eruption tell and homing speed so it reads as a beatable ambush rather than an unavoidable floor-spawn.

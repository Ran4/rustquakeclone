# QUAKECLONE — Feature Ideas

56 innovative feature proposals, each in its own file. Pick one up, build it, then move its file to `implemented/` (see `CLAUDE.md`).

Generated by a multi-agent brainstorm (12 creative lenses → curated to the best three dozen, then a second wave of 20 more).

## Movement

- **01. [Grapnel Whip — Weapon 7 Becomes a Swing Verb](01-grapnel-whip-swing-verb.md)** — Alt-fire the Whip as a hitscan grapple that reels you in and pendulum-swings you across the brushwork, turning weapon 7 from a melee shove into a whole new traversal verb.
- **02. [Slipstream Carve — Surf the Wall Normals](02-slipstream-carve-surf-the-wall-normals.md)** — Any tilted brush becomes a Source-style surf ramp by feeding the solver's own wall normal into a gravity-projection branch that bleeds your fall into forward speed.
- **03. [Telefrag Nail — The Nailgun Blink-Dash](03-telefrag-nail-blink-dash.md)** — A charged nail plants a recoverable beacon — even on the moving truck deck — and a second tap swept-teleports your whole AABB there, carrying your velocity straight across a void rift.
- **04. [Momentum Bank — Spend Speed You Never Stopped Earning](04-momentum-bank.md)** — A meter that quietly fills while you keep your speed high, then dumps it all in one explosive kinetic burst the moment you choose to cash out.
- **37. [Wall-Run — Hold the Wall and Sprint the Skybox](37-wall-run-sprint-the-skybox.md)** — Press into a wall at speed and run flat along it for a stamina window, gravity along the face cancelled, so a wall-run → wall-jump → grapple chain turns the skybox into a route.
- **38. [Terminal Velocity — Fall Damage Becomes a Weapon](38-terminal-velocity-fall-damage-weapon.md)** — A mid-air slam drives you straight down and converts the height you fell into a radial ground-pound shockwave, scaling damage and knockback with impact speed just like a truck ram.

## Combat

- **05. [Dismemberment Nailgun — Per-Bone Crippling](05-dismemberment-nailgun-per-bone-crippling.md)** — The Nailgun stops shooting at monsters and starts shooting at their limbs — sever the sword-arm to disarm a Knight, cripple a leg to outrun it, or pop the head for a free kill.
- **06. [The Lodestone — Gravity-Well Grenade](06-lodestone-gravity-well-grenade.md)** — A grenade alt-fire that turns its blast inside-out — for two seconds it sucks every monster and loose projectile into one screaming clump, so the rocket you fire next gibs the whole pile at once.
- **07. [Phantom Echo — Time-Delayed Damage Stamp](07-phantom-echo-time-delayed-damage-stamp.md)** — Crack the Whip to drop a ghost anchor; 1.2s later every hit you landed in that window detonates again at its recorded position, rewarding commit-then-reposition flurries.
- **08. [Conductive Lightning — Arcs That Find Their Path](08-conductive-lightning.md)** — The Lightning Gun stops being a single beam and becomes a conduction solver — its arc jumps through packed, metal-armored, and sludge-standing enemies, frying whole clusters at once.
- **39. [Whip Parry — Bat the Bolt Back](39-whip-parry-bat-the-bolt-back.md)** — A timed Whip swing swats an incoming bolt, spit or grenade out of the air and reflects it back down the shooter's throat, giving the no-ammo melee weapon a defensive identity.
- **40. [Cryo Shatter — Freeze a Rig Solid, Then Break It](40-cryo-shatter-freeze-a-rig-solid.md)** — Chill a monster until its animator stops and it freezes into a brittle ice statue — bonus damage, shatters into gibs from any touch, and a temporary solid you can stand on while frozen.
- **41. [Nail Pin — Stake Them to the Wall](41-nail-pin-stake-them-to-the-wall.md)** — A nail that catches a monster against a surface stakes it there, rooting a Scrag to the ceiling or a Knight to a pillar as it struggles free — and pins a live one into the lava to cook.

## Enemies

- **09. [The Weaver — Spider That Spins Walkable Web Bridges](09-the-weaver-spider-web-bridges.md)** — A new arachnid rig anchors taut silk strands that reserve moving-collider slots, becoming real surfaces the solver, pathing, hitscan and LoS all respect — sever one to drop everything riding it.
- **10. [The Choir — A Swarm That Fuses Into One Rig](10-the-choir-swarm-fuses-into-one-rig.md)** — A swarm of tiny motes physically grafts its bone hierarchies onto a shared root to assemble one bigger, slower brute — and when it dies it fissions back into scattering motes that re-form elsewhere.
- **11. [Adrenal Conductor — Heartbeat-Scrubbed Director](11-adrenal-conductor-heartbeat-director.md)** — An AI Director that retimes spawns and the Idle→Chase→Attack machine to a live threat budget, and scrubs a synthesised heartbeat's BPM in real time so the soundtrack literally IS the difficulty curve.
- **12. [Pack Doctrine — Knights That Flank on a Blackboard](12-pack-doctrine-knights-flank-blackboard.md)** — A squad blackboard splits Knights into roles — a baiter holds your gaze while flankers reverse-LoS into your blind arc and a reserve only commits once two are engaged, turning brushwork into pincers.
- **42. [The Mimic — The Medkit Bites Back](42-the-mimic-the-medkit-bites-back.md)** — A rare monster disguised as a pickup that sits still until you reach for it, then blooms its bone rig and lunges — poisoning the one un-punished reflex in the genre: grabbing loot.
- **43. [The Burrower — It Comes Up Through the Floor](43-the-burrower-up-through-the-floor.md)** — A digger that tracks you unseen beneath the brushwork as a tremor and dust-trail, then erupts directly under your feet for an ambush before submerging to reposition.
- **44. [The Sapper — It Eats Your Ammo](44-the-sapper-it-eats-your-ammo.md)** — A skittering thief that latches on, drains your ammo or jams your weapon, and bolts to stash the haul — kill it to reclaim the drop, or watch your rockets run off down the corridor.

## Vehicles

- **13. [Floodgate Lift — A Weight-Driven Platform You Fight On](13-floodgate-lift-weight-driven-platform.md)** — A counterweighted freight deck that rises and falls by the literal weight of bodies on it — shoot the pin to plummet, or pile gibbed corpses on the pan to climb a dam shaft.
- **14. [Powder Keg — Throwable, Rideable, Rammable Barrel](14-powder-keg-throwable-rideable-rammable-barrel.md)** — A black-powder barrel that is rideable, punt-able and rammable all at once — and a lit fuse turns a rolling keg into a daisy-chaining bowling-ball cluster bomb.
- **15. [Hijack the Horde — Mount and Drive a Stunned Ogre](15-hijack-the-horde-mount-and-drive-a-stunned-ogre.md)** — Whip-stagger an Ogre, mount its shoulders, and drive the bruiser itself as a living, chainsaw-revving ram until it dies and bucks you off.
- **45. [Mine Cart — The Rails Decide, Until You Blow Them](45-mine-cart-the-rails-decide.md)** — A rideable cart bound to an authored rail spline that you fight from and brake by leaning — until you shoot out a rail or a switch to redirect or derail it into free physics.
- **46. [The Bed Gun — A Pintle Turret on the Flatbed](46-the-bed-gun-pintle-turret-on-the-flatbed.md)** — A swivel cannon bolted to the truck's deck that you mount like the driver spot and aim independently of the truck's heading, turning the ram into a rolling weapons platform.

## Systems

- **16. [Spreading Fire — Lava That Learns to Climb](16-spreading-fire-lava-that-learns-to-climb.md)** — Hazard volumes become a living cellular automaton — splash and burning gibs ignite flammable cells that crawl fire up wood catwalks and across sludge to clear, or seal, a room.
- **17. [Ragdoll Physics — Corpses Flop and Fall](17-ragdoll-physics-corpses-flop-and-fall.md)** — A clean kill hands the monster's skeleton to a Verlet ragdoll: it topples under gravity, limbs flopping, drapes over whatever it lands on, and stays solid enough to bump, stand on, and shove (or boot into the lava).
- **18. [Pressure & Flood — The Dam Actually Breaks](18-pressure-and-flood-the-dam-actually-breaks.md)** — Breach the spillway gate and watch the reservoir drop in real time while the gorge floods, re-shaping the lethal collision zone mid-fight.
- **47. [Material Floors — The Ground Has Opinions](47-material-floors-the-ground-has-opinions.md)** — Every surface gets a material that bends movement — ice slips, tar drags, grates shove — generalising level 2's ice into a per-theme rule sampled under your feet each frame.
- **48. [Updrafts & Gales — Wind That Shoves Everything](48-updrafts-and-gales-wind-that-shoves.md)** — Directional force volumes — lava thermals, gorge crosswinds, hive vents — that push the player, projectiles, gibs and the truck alike, making air control a negotiation with the room.
- **49. [Killlights — Shoot the Lights Out](49-killlights-shoot-the-lights-out.md)** — The lamps lighting each room are destructible; shoot one and the area drops to fog-black, and since both your sight and the monsters' perception hinge on it, the dark becomes a weapon.

## Environmental

- **19. [Slipgate Echo — Momentum-Preserving Portals](19-slipgate-echo-momentum-portals.md)** — Paired Build-API portals teleport you with your velocity rotated into the exit frame, forwarding hitscan and line-of-sight straight through the link so you bunny-hop in one mouth and rocket out the other.
- **20. [Gravity Shrines — Walk the Walls to the Key](20-gravity-shrines-walk-the-walls.md)** — Step into a shrine's field and "down" rotates onto the nearest wall — so you walk, jump and fight your way up an Escher box to a key no floor can reach.
- **21. [The Resonant Vault — Ring the Bells in Order](21-resonant-vault-ring-the-bells-in-order.md)** — A key-vault that opens only when you strike its faceted bell-pillars in the right order — and the room's ambient drone is quietly humming you the combination.
- **22. [The Tide Engine — A Rising Lethal Sea](22-tide-engine-rising-lethal-sea.md)** — A lava/sludge/void sea whose surface sine-scrubs up and down every frame — a moving collider that lifts riders like an elevator, gates trenches, and only burns when it rises above your feet.
- **50. [Collapse — The Floor Is Leaving](50-collapse-the-floor-is-leaving.md)** — A forward-only chase where brushes detach and fall away behind you on a timer or your own footsteps, turning the dam finale into a run you survive on foot or in the truck.
- **51. [Bounce Brushes — Quake Jump Pads, Weaponised](51-bounce-brushes-jump-pads-weaponised.md)** — The classic jump pad made universal: a brush that flings anything landing on it — you, a grenade, a barrel, a Knight, the truck — so you chain pads, bank shots, and boot monsters into the lava.

## Meta

- **23. [The Sigil Pact — Pay-in-Flesh Mutators](23-sigil-pact-pay-in-flesh-mutators.md)** — At every exit slipgate, three sigils offer to make you stronger — and the next dimension darker — so the eight hand-built maps re-roll into a personalised, escalating curse.
- **24. [Splice — Looted Monster Bones Become Weapon Mods](24-splice-monster-bones-weapon-mods.md)** — Blow a limb off a monster, loot the bone, and bolt that creature's behaviour onto your gun.
- **25. [Echo Phantoms — Your Last Death Haunts the Run](25-echo-phantoms.md)** — Every level you enter is already occupied by a solid ghost of your last attempt — walking your old route, firing your old shots, and standing guard exactly where you died.
- **26. [Overclock Gauntlet — The Truck Is the Run Clock](26-overclock-gauntlet-truck-run-clock.md)** — The flatbed rides with you through all eight dimensions on a draining overclock core — bank leftover charge at each exit while mounted, and the engine-rev pitch becomes your tension meter.
- **53. [Style Engine — The Announcer Rates Your Carnage](53-style-engine-the-announcer-rates-your-carnage.md)** — A Devil-May-Cry carnage meter that rewards varied kills — weapons, airborne frags, rams, parries — with an escalating announcer and payoff, and drains when you repeat a trick or take a hit.

## Audio

- **27. [The Listening Dark — Echo-Ping Echolocation](27-listening-dark-echo-ping-echolocation.md)** — A no-ammo pulse you fire into the black that paints the room back to you as a fan of delayed clicks — timing, pan and pitch spelling out distance, direction, and whether you pinged wall, lava or a breathing rig.
- **28. [Noise Budget — Diegetic Sound Stealth](28-noise-budget-diegetic-sound-stealth.md)** — Every sound the player makes already has a volume and a position — let the monsters hear it, so a sloppy bunny-hop landing wakes the room before anyone sees you.
- **29. [Resonant Brushwork — Sonic Demolition](29-resonant-brushwork-sonic-demolition.md)** — Every surface has a ringing pitch, and holding the Lightning beam on a brush sweeps it up to its shatter note to crack open a shortcut.
- **52. [Cathedral Verb — The Room Shapes the Sound](52-cathedral-verb-the-room-shapes-the-sound.md)** — A handful of rays gauge the room's size each frame and drive a procedural reverb, so a shotgun is a dry crack in a vent and a rolling boom on the dam crest.

## Rendering

- **30. [Palette Quantizer — The Dimension Decides the Colors](30-palette-quantizer.md)** — Snap the whole frame to a tiny per-dimension indexed palette before bloom, so only lava, pickups and muzzle flashes are allowed to bleed past into white-hot HDR.
- **31. [Damage Decay — Wounds Carved Into the Rig's Facets](31-damage-decay-wounds-carved-into-facets.md)** — Every hit caves a dent into the exact bone you struck, so a monster wears its wounds in its own facets and gibs when those facets finally invert.
- **32. [Volumetric Slabs — Fog You Can Shoot a Hole In](32-volumetric-slabs-fog-you-can-shoot-a-hole-in.md)** — Drifting fog-slabs you can punch a temporary glowing clear-disc through with a rocket or the lightning beam, exposing the monsters lurking inside.
- **33. [Slipgate Bleed — The Next Dimension Leaks Backward](33-slipgate-bleed-next-dimension-leaks-backward.md)** — The exit slipgate sweats the next level's wrong-colored fog a few meters into the current room, so a transition becomes a gradient you walk through instead of a hard cut.
- **54. [PSX Wobble — The Renderer Remembers 1996](54-psx-wobble-the-renderer-remembers-1996.md)** — A retro render mode that snaps vertices to a coarse grid, warps textures with affine mapping and upscales a low-res buffer, leaning the already-faceted art all the way into a lost-disc look.
- **55. [Carnage Stains — The Room Remembers Every Kill](55-carnage-stains-the-room-remembers.md)** — Blood, scorch rings, pellet pocks and corpse-drag smears accumulate as persistent decals, so a cleared arena reads as a battlefield — the world's record of violence, not the rig's.

## Creation

- **34. [Brushwright — In-World Map Forge](34-brushwright-in-world-map-forge.md)** — Hit a key, the world freezes, and a glowing grid cursor lets you stamp real Build brushes into the map — collision, fog and texel density correct for free.
- **56. [The Infinite Slipgate — Endless Procedural Dimensions](56-the-infinite-slipgate-endless-procedural-dimensions.md)** — Past level 8 the slipgate keeps opening onto seeded, procedurally-assembled dimensions stitched from the Build API and dressed in a rolling theme, forever.

## Social

- **35. [Daily Dimension — Shared Seed + Async Leaderboard](35-daily-dimension-shared-seed-async-leaderboard.md)** — A seed-of-the-day perturbs every monster, key and item placement so the whole world plays the same hand-built map with the same twist, then races it on a victory-screen async board.

## Wild

- **36. [The Cartographer — Whip a Wall Out of the World](36-the-cartographer-whip-a-wall-out-of-the-world.md)** — A Whip alt-fire rips a brush loose from the level, turning a wall into a platform, a ramp, or a corpse-pulping wrecking ball you swing on a leash.

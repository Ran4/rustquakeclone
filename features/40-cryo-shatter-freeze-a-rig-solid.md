# 40. Cryo Shatter — Freeze a Rig Solid, Then Break It

> Chill it until the animation stops, then watch it stand there as glass — until anything at all touches it.

**The idea** — A new **cold** damage type — cryo nails, or a cell-fed freeze beam — pours *chill* into a monster instead of red damage, building a per-monster chill meter. Cross the threshold and the rig **flash-freezes**: the procedural animator halts mid-stride, the monster locks into whatever pose it was holding, and it becomes a brittle ice statue. While frozen it takes a massive bonus-damage multiplier and **shatters into gibs from literally any contact** — one shotgun pellet, a rocket's splash, a truck ram, even a falling brush. Crucially it's also a *temporary solid surface*: a frozen Knight is cover you can crouch behind, or a stepping stone to a ledge — until something cracks it.

**Why it's fresh** — Ice in shooters is usually a slow debuff or a death animation; here freezing is a real *state change* that turns a living combatant into level geometry. The same rig is enemy, then obstacle, then a one-hit pinata — and because the skeleton *is* the entity, the frozen pose is genuinely whatever it was doing, sword raised or mid-lunge. No other Quake mechanic lets you weaponise the shatter chain: freeze a clustered pack, then crack one and let splash detonate the brittle rest.

**How it plays** — Cryo asks a two-beat question instead of one: spend the chill to lock a threat, then *finish* it with anything cheap to hand. You can flash-freeze the charging Death Knight, walk behind its frozen bulk for cover, then super-shotgun it to glass. Or freeze a Scrag mid-air and watch it hang there as a free stepping stone, knowing a stray nail will drop it. The new tension is timing the shatter — break it too early and you waste the multiplier, too late and it thaws back to a very angry monster.

**How it fits QUAKECLONE** — Freezing is cheap because `monster_model.rs` is already a parented bone hierarchy driven each frame: we just *pause* the procedural animator on a frozen flag and swap the rig's faceted materials to a frosted emissive blue that `HDR + bloom` renders as glass for free. A chill accumulator lives beside the `enemies.rs` Idle→Chase→Attack state as a fourth `Frozen` state that gates all AI and attacks. Shattering routes straight into the existing `combat.rs` death/gib path with the bonus multiplier applied, and `effects.rs` adds the ice-shard burst on top of the normal gibs. The "frozen-as-cover" trick borrows `vehicle.rs`'s moving-collider idea — the rig reserves one `WorldColliders.solids` slot from its AABB while frozen — so `physics.rs` `move_and_slide`, hitscan and `Player.vel` step-up treat the statue as solid with no new code. Cryo nails and the freeze beam slot into `weapons.rs`/`projectiles.rs` as a cold-damage variant.

**Build sketch** — Add a `Chill` component and a cold `DamageEvent` flavour; when chill caps, set `Frozen { thaw_timer }`, pause the animator, swap materials, and push a solids slot. Any `DamageEvent` on a frozen rig (or a contact test against the truck/falling-brush colliders) triggers `combat.rs` gibbing with the multiplier; the thaw timer reverts the state if nothing hits it. The honest hard part is the freeze→shatter→thaw state machine and registering the rig as a stable solid without the static-collider assumption deadlocking `enemies.rs` pathing or wedging the player inside a statue.

**Effort** — **M.** Main risk: the freeze→shatter state machine and making a frozen rig a stable solid without deadlocking the AI or trapping the player.
